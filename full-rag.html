RAG：面向知识密集型 NLP 任务的检索增强生成

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks · Lewis et al., NeurIPS 2020 · 原文 PDF（本站内置，可分屏阅读） · arXiv:2005.11401

摘要

核心论点
"Large pre-trained language models have been shown to store factual knowledge in their parameters, and achieve state-of-the-art results when fine-tuned on downstream NLP tasks. However, their ability to access and precisely manipulate knowledge is still limited, and hence on knowledge-intensive tasks, their performance lags behind task-specific architectures. Additionally, providing provenance for their decisions and updating their world knowledge remain open research problems. ... We explore a general-purpose fine-tuning recipe for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) — models which combine pre-trained parametric and non-parametric memory for language generation. ... For language generation tasks, we find that RAG models generate more specific, diverse and factual language than a state-of-the-art parametric-only seq2seq baseline."
大型预训练语言模型已被证明能把事实知识存进参数，并在下游 NLP 任务微调后取得最优结果。然而它们访问与精确操纵知识的能力仍然有限，因此在知识密集型任务上落后于专用架构；此外，如何为决策提供出处（provenance）、如何更新其世界知识，仍是开放问题。我们探索检索增强生成（RAG）的通用微调配方——组合参数记忆与非参数记忆的语言生成模型：参数记忆是预训练 seq2seq 模型，非参数记忆是 Wikipedia 的稠密向量索引，由预训练神经检索器访问。我们在多个知识密集型任务上微调并评估，在三个开放域问答任务上刷新纪录，超越纯参数 seq2seq 模型与专用的「检索-抽取」架构；在生成任务上，RAG 生成的内容比纯参数基线更具体、更多样、更符合事实。
解读：摘要里埋着今天知识库系统的三大卖点——可更新（索引换了知识就换）、可溯源（生成有出处）、不幻觉（更符合事实）。这三个词就是第 4 章的目录。

§1 引言

核心论点 · 参数记忆的三个缺点
"Pre-trained neural language models have been shown to learn a substantial amount of in-depth knowledge from data. They can do so without any access to an external memory, as a parameterized implicit knowledge base. While this development is exciting, such models do have downsides: They cannot easily expand or revise their memory, can’t straightforwardly provide insight into their predictions, and may produce “hallucinations”. Hybrid models that combine parametric memory with non-parametric (i.e., retrieval-based) memories can address some of these issues because knowledge can be directly revised and expanded, and accessed knowledge can be inspected and interpreted."
预训练神经语言模型能从数据中学到大量深层知识，不需要任何外部记忆，就像一个「参数化的隐式知识库」。但这种模型有缺点：它们无法轻松扩展或修改自己的记忆，无法直接解释预测的依据，还可能产生「幻觉」。把参数记忆与非参数（即检索式）记忆结合的混合模型可以缓解这些问题——知识可以直接修订与扩展，被检索到的知识也可以被检查与解释。
解读：三个缺点对应三种工程动作：不能扩展 → 建索引随时加文档（第 4 章入库管道）；不能解释 → 片段带来源进上下文（引用）；会幻觉 → 检索接地 + 「未找到就说未找到」。第 4 章整章都是对这三个缺点的工程回应。
方法机制 · 组合与端到端
"We build RAG models where the parametric memory is a pre-trained seq2seq transformer, and the non-parametric memory is a dense vector index of Wikipedia, accessed with a pre-trained neural retriever. We combine these components in a probabilistic model trained end-to-end. The retriever (Dense Passage Retriever, henceforth DPR) provides latent documents conditioned on the input, and the seq2seq model (BART) then conditions on these latent documents together with the input to generate the output. We marginalize the latent documents with a top-K approximation, either on a per-output basis (assuming the same document is responsible for all tokens) or a per-token basis (where different documents are responsible for different tokens). ... There has been extensive previous work proposing architectures to enrich systems with non-parametric memory which are trained from scratch for specific tasks ... In contrast, we explore a setting where both parametric and non-parametric memory components are pre-trained and pre-loaded with extensive knowledge. Crucially, by using pre-trained access mechanisms, the ability to access knowledge is present without additional training."
我们构建的 RAG 模型中，参数记忆是预训练的 seq2seq Transformer，非参数记忆是 Wikipedia 的稠密向量索引，由预训练神经检索器访问。两者组合成一个端到端训练的概率模型：检索器（DPR，稠密段落检索器）根据输入给出潜在文档，seq2seq 模型（BART）以这些潜在文档与输入为条件生成输出。我们对潜在文档做 top-K 近似边缘化——或按整段输出（同一文档负责全部 token），或按每个 token（不同文档负责不同 token）。与此前从零训练的记忆网络不同，RAG 的两个记忆组件都是预训练并预载了大量知识的——关键是，凭借预训练的访问机制，「访问知识的能力」无需额外训练就已具备。
实验结论 · 摘要级战报
"Our RAG models achieve state-of-the-art results on open Natural Questions, WebQuestions and CuratedTrec and strongly outperform recent approaches that use specialised pre-training objectives on TriviaQA. Despite these being extractive tasks, we find that unconstrained generation outperforms previous extractive approaches. For knowledge-intensive generation, we experiment with MS-MARCO and Jeopardy question generation, and we find that our models generate responses that are more factual, specific, and diverse than a BART baseline. For FEVER fact verification, we achieve results within 4.3% of state-of-the-art pipeline models which use strong retrieval supervision. Finally, we demonstrate that the non-parametric memory can be replaced to update the models’ knowledge as the world changes."
RAG 在开放版 Natural Questions、WebQuestions、CuratedTrec 上刷新纪录，并在 TriviaQA 上大幅超越使用专门预训练目标的方法。尽管这些是抽取式任务，我们发现不受约束的生成反而超越了以往的抽取式方法。生成任务上，RAG 的回答比 BART 基线更符合事实、更具体、更多样。FEVER 事实验证上与最强流水线系统差距 4.3% 以内（对方用了强检索监督）。最后我们证明：世界变化时，只需替换非参数记忆即可更新模型知识。

§2 方法

方法机制 · 两个变体
"We explore RAG models, which use the input sequence x to retrieve text documents z and use them as additional context when generating the target sequence y. ... To train the retriever and generator end-to-end, we treat the retrieved document as a latent variable. We propose two models that marginalize over the latent documents in different ways to produce a distribution over generated text. In one approach, RAG-Sequence, the model uses the same document to predict each target token. The second approach, RAG-Token, can predict each target token based on a different document."
RAG 用输入序列 x 检索文档 z，并在生成目标序列 y 时把它们作为额外上下文。检索到的文档被当作潜在变量，两种边缘化方式对应两个模型：RAG-Sequence——整段生成使用同一份文档（对整段概率做边缘化）；RAG-Token——每个目标 token 可以基于不同的文档（逐 token 边缘化）。把类别视为长度为 1 的目标序列时，RAG 也可用于分类任务，此时两个变体等价。
解读：用大白话记：RAG-Sequence 是「查一次资料写整篇」，RAG-Token 是「每写一个词都可以换一份资料」。后者更强但更贵——下文 Jeopardy 实验会看到它赢在哪。
方法机制 · 三组件
"The retrieval component pη(z|x) is based on DPR. DPR follows a bi-encoder architecture: d(z) is a dense representation of a document produced by a BERT document encoder, and q(x) a query representation produced by a query encoder ... Calculating the list of k documents with highest prior probability is a Maximum Inner Product Search (MIPS) problem, which can be approximately solved in sub-linear time. ... The generator component could be modelled using any encoder-decoder. We use BART-large, a pre-trained seq2seq transformer with 400M parameters. To combine the input x with the retrieved content z when generating from BART, we simply concatenate them. ... We jointly train the retriever and generator components without any direct supervision on what document should be retrieved. ... Updating the document encoder during training is costly as it requires the document index to be periodically updated as REALM does during pre-training. We do not find this step necessary for strong performance, and keep the document encoder (and index) fixed, only fine-tuning the query encoder and the BART generator."
检索器：基于 DPR 双塔架构——文档编码器与查询编码器各把文本编码成稠密向量，取内积最高的 top-K 文档（MIPS 最大内积搜索，可亚线性近似求解）。生成器：BART-large（4 亿参数的预训练 seq2seq），把输入 x 与检索内容 z 拼接后生成。文档索引：即「非参数记忆」。训练时不对「该检索什么文档」做任何直接监督，只最小化目标答案的负对数似然，联合训练检索器与生成器；为省成本，文档编码器与索引保持冻结，只微调查询编码器与生成器。
解读：「冻结文档编码器也能取得强效果」是个被忽视的工程利好：换新文档不用重训任何东西——这就是今天「换索引即换知识」的技术依据。第 4 章的入库管道之所以能分钟级生效，源头在此。
可跳读 · §2.5 解码
"At test time, RAG-Sequence and RAG-Token require different ways to approximate arg max p(y|x). RAG-Token ... can be plugged into a standard beam decoder. For RAG-Sequence, the likelihood p(y|x) does not break into a conventional per-token likelihood, hence we cannot solve it with a single beam search. Instead, we run beam search for each document z ... We refer to this decoding procedure as “Thorough Decoding.” ... We refer to this decoding procedure as “Fast Decoding.”"
RAG-Token 可直接接入标准束搜索；RAG-Sequence 因似然不按 token 分解，需要对每份文档分别束搜索再汇总（Thorough Decoding），或用近似省掉额外前向（Fast Decoding）。实现细节，不影响理解思想。

§3 实验设置

方法机制 · 语料与任务
"Following Lee et al. and Karpukhin et al., we use the December 2018 dump. Each Wikipedia article is split into disjoint 100-word chunks, to make a total of 21M documents. We use the document encoder to compute an embedding for each document, and build a single MIPS index using FAISS ... During training, we retrieve the top k documents for each query. ... Crucially, unlike most other approaches to FEVER, we do not use supervision on retrieved evidence. In many real-world applications, retrieval supervision signals aren’t available, and models that do not require such supervision will be applicable to a wider range of tasks."
非参数知识源是 2018 年 12 月的 Wikipedia 转储：每篇文章切成互不重叠的 100 词块，共 2100 万份文档，用 FAISS 建立检索索引。覆盖五类任务：开放域问答（NQ / TriviaQA / WQ / CuratedTrec）、抽象式问答（MS-MARCO，不用金标段落）、Jeopardy 问题生成（从事实线索反推实体，如从「1986 年墨西哥成为首个两度举办该赛事的国家」猜「世界杯」）、FEVER 事实验证（三分类的标签被映射成单个输出 token，且不使用检索证据监督——现实中检索监督往往不存在）。
解读：「100 词一块、2100 万块」——第 4 章切块参数（200~500 token）的学术原型就在这。而「不用检索监督」的主张，正是今天「零标注即可上 RAG」的信心来源。

解读：「11.8% vs 0%」是全文最容易被忽略的金句：检索与参数记忆不是二选一，它们的协作能产出任何单独一方都给不出的答案。下文海明威案例会把这件事讲得可视化。

"Evaluators indicated that BART was more factual than RAG in only 7.1% of cases, while RAG was more factual in 42.7% of cases, and both RAG and BART were factual in a further 17% of cases, clearly demonstrating the effectiveness of RAG on the task over a state-of-the-art generation model. 方法机制 · 「海明威」案例：两种记忆如何协作（Figure 2）
"When generating “Sun”, the posterior is high for document 2 which mentions “The Sun Also Rises”. Similarly, document 1 dominates the posterior when “A Farewell to Arms” is generated. Intriguingly, after the first token of each book is generated, the document posterior flattens. This observation suggests that the generator can complete the titles without depending on specific documents. In other words, the model’s parametric knowledge is sufficient to complete the titles. We find evidence for this hypothesis by feeding the BART-only baseline with the partial decoding ... This example shows how parametric and non-parametric memories work together — the non-parametric component helps to guide the generation, drawing out specific knowledge stored in the parametric memory."
生成 Jeopardy 问题「"太阳照常升起"是这位作者的小说」的答案「Hemingway」时，RAG-Token 检索到 5 份文档。逐 token 观察文档后验分布：生成「与武器告别」（A Farewell to Arms）时文档 1 的后验最高（该文档提到这部 1929 年的小说），生成「太阳照常升起」（The Sun Also Rises）时文档 2 的后验最高（该文档提到这部 1926 年的处女作）。耐人寻味的是：每本书的书名开出第一个词之后，文档后验就变平了——说明模型靠参数记忆就能补完书名（作者用只喂部分前缀的 BART 验证了这一点）。非参数记忆负责把生成引向正确的知识区域，参数记忆负责在其中流畅地补完细节——两种记忆各司其职地协作。
解读：这是全文最美的实验：它把「检索管找对方向、参数管补完细节」画成了一张逐 token 的后验图。第 4 章「检索层供材料、生成层做忠实转述」的分工，在这里有了逐字证据。

§4.4 事实验证

实验结论
"For 3-way classification, RAG scores are within 4.3% of state-of-the-art models, which are complex pipeline systems with domain-specific architectures and substantial engineering, trained using intermediate retrieval supervision, which RAG does not require. For 2-way classification, we compare against Thorne and Vlachos, who train RoBERTa to classify the claim as true or false given the gold evidence sentence. RAG achieves an accuracy within 2.7% of this model, despite being supplied with only the claim and retrieving its own evidence. ... We find that the top retrieved document is from a gold article in 71% of cases, and a gold article is present in the top 10 retrieved articles in 90% of cases."
FEVER 三分类上 RAG 与最强系统差距 4.3% 以内——对方是带领域架构与大量工程、且使用中间检索监督的复杂流水线，RAG 两者都不需要；二分类上与用金标证据句训练的 RoBERTa 分类器差距 2.7%。检索质量分析：Top-1 检索文档与 FEVER 金标证据文章的重合率 71%，Top-10 内含金标文章的比例 90%。

§4.5 附加结果：三组消融（Builder 最该细读的部分）

实验结论 · Table 6（学习式检索 vs 冻结 vs BM25）
"A key feature of RAG is learning to retrieve relevant information for the task. To assess the effectiveness of the retrieval mechanism, we run ablations where we freeze the retriever during training. As shown in Table 6, learned retrieval improves results for all tasks. We compare RAG’s dense retriever to a word overlap-based BM25 retriever. ... For FEVER, BM25 performs best, perhaps since FEVER claims are heavily entity-centric and thus well-suited for word overlap-based retrieval. Differentiable retrieval improves results on all other tasks, especially for Open-Domain QA, where it is crucial. ... An advantage of non-parametric memory models like RAG is that knowledge can be easily updated at test time. Parametric-only models like T5 or BART need further training to update their behavior as the world changes. ... RAG answers 70% correctly using the 2016 index for 2016 world leaders and 68% using the 2018 index for 2018 world leaders. Accuracy with mismatched indices is low (12% with the 2018 index and 2016 leaders, 4% with the 2016 index and 2018 leaders). This shows we can update RAG’s world knowledge by simply replacing its non-parametric memory."
① 学习式检索有效：在全部任务上，联合训练的检索优于冻结检索（如 NQ：RAG-Seq 学习式 44.0 vs 冻结 41.2）。② 与 BM25 对比分任务而论：BM25 只在 FEVER 上最好（FEVER 论断高度实体中心、适合词面匹配），其余任务（尤其开放域问答）都是稠密检索占优——第 4 章「混合检索」主张的实验依据。③ 索引热替换（Index hot-swapping）：用 2016 年 12 月与 2018 年 12 月两版 Wikipedia 索引，对 82 位在这两个时点间变更的世界领袖提问「谁是现任 {职位}」——索引匹配时正确率 70%/68%，错配时仅 12%/4%。结论：把世界知识更新，只需替换非参数记忆。
解读：「热替换」实验是「知识更新 = 换索引而非换参数」最直观的证明——也是第 4 章四种知识来源表里「知识库：增删文档即生效」的出处。而 70% vs 12% 的悬殊对比，比任何说教都更能向业务方解释「为什么知识库必须做版本管理」（第 4 章失败模式 ③ 的原型）。
可跳读
"Models are trained with either 5 or 10 retrieved latent documents, and we do not observe significant differences in performance between them. We have the flexibility to adjust the number of retrieved documents at test time, which can affect performance and runtime. Figure 3 (left) shows that retrieving more documents at test time monotonically improves Open-domain QA results for RAG-Sequence, but performance peaks for RAG-Token at 10 retrieved documents."
§4.5 末「检索文档数 K 的影响」：测试时增大 K 对 RAG-Sequence 单调提升、RAG-Token 在 K=10 处达峰——知道「K 是可调的测试时参数」即可。

§5–6 相关工作与结论（速览）

可跳读 · 定位与总结
"Prior work has shown that retrieval improves performance across a variety of NLP tasks when considered in isolation. ... Our work unifies previous successes in incorporating retrieval into individual tasks, showing that a single retrieval-based architecture is capable of achieving strong performance across several tasks. ... A key feature of our memory is that it is comprised of raw text rather distributed representations, which makes the memory both (i) human-readable, lending a form of interpretability to our model, and (ii) human-writable, enabling us to dynamically update the model’s memory by editing the document index. ...
